Shared patterns

Modal

A centered dialog over a dimmed, blurred scrim — used for "report a problem" and "delete" confirmations. The scrim is absolutely positioned inside anyposition: relative container (the real page frame, in practice); the panel itself is capped at the system's 440px modal width.
